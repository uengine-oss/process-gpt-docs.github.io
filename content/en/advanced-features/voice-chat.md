# Voice Agent and Document/Slide Generation

ProcessGPT provides an autonomous work environment that completes business processes through voice instructions alone — without screen touches or complex typing — and instantly generates and edits a wide range of document outputs (Word, HWP, PPT, etc.) tailored to the company's standard forms.


## 1.1 Viewing Process Lists

The method of checking the list of generated processes using voice chat is as follows.

1. Click the 'Chat' icon at the top to enter the chat screen, then verify that an Assistant has been created. (If it has not been created, add System as a participant via chat-room creation and verify that the screen appears.)
2. Click the 'Speak/Listen' icon at the bottom of the message input area to enter the voice chat screen.

![](../../../uengine-image/process-gpt/voice-chat/2.png)

3. Click the microphone button and conduct a voice conversation asking about the process you want to check, such as *"Explain the contract management process among the generated processes."*
4. Once the conversation is complete, the AI will, after a brief moment, provide voice answers about each Task and the connected Form information for the contract management process among the registered processes, and you can also confirm the AI's answer in the text chat.

![](../../../uengine-image/process-gpt/voice-chat/4.png)<br><br><br>


## 1.2 Process Planning

The method of planning a new process such as a vacation request process using voice chat is as follows.

1. Click the 'Speak/Listen' icon at the bottom of the message input area to enter the voice chat screen.
2. Click the microphone button and make a request about the process you want to plan, such as "Plan the detailed Tasks for planning a vacation request process."
3. Tip: The more specific the requirements you add, the more detailed the Task planning will be, so it is recommended to describe the process requirements in detail.
4. Once the conversation is complete, the AI will provide step-by-step voice answers based on the detailed Tasks and Form contents of the vacation request process, and you can also check the AI's answer in the chat window.

Through this, you can quickly understand information generated within the company and receive AI-supported guidance on the detailed steps during process planning, improving work efficiency and raising the quality of your processes.

## 1.4 Intelligent Output Completion: Production-Grade Documents Without the "AI Look" (Zero-Touch Polish)

The days of switching between multiple tools to create a single report and manually reworking AI outputs piece by piece are over. ProcessGPT has evolved beyond an "AI that just creates documents" into an "AI that completes production-ready documents together with you, right inside the chat window."

### 1.4.1 Unbroken Flow: Conclude Even PPTX in a Single Conversation

From planning to visualization, everything is wrapped up in a single conversation without switching tools. Building on existing HWPX and DOCX support, presentation (PPTX) slide drafts can now be generated and immediately reviewed in the artifact view. There is no need to open another program just to turn a document into slides.

- **Global Edit Instruction:** Prompts like *"Make this entire slide a brighter mood"* enable uninterrupted, conversation-only editing — no need to regenerate the whole deck.
- **Regional Precise Editing:** Drag the mouse over only the specific region you want to fix on the screen, and refine that area with pinpoint precision.
- **Automatic Image Composition:** Select a specific region in the slide and upload a photo, and the image is instantly composited into that location.

<br>

### 1.4.2 Production-Grade Detail (Zero-Touch Polish): Submit Even Large Proposals As-Is

The quality is so polished it appears human-crafted and ready for immediate submission upon generation. Tone and structure are automatically applied according to the document type, even for complex 40-plus-page proposals or reports.

- **Perfect Table Comprehension:** Through HWPX form snapshot analysis, even complex tables are filled in accurately without structural collapse.
- **Automatic Indentation and Alignment:** Symbol-by-symbol spacing is automatically aligned cleanly, just as if a human had pressed Shift+Tab manually.
- **Smart Resizing & Caption Summarization:** Images are automatically adjusted to the width/height ratio of the insertion space, and lengthy image or table descriptions are crisply summarized into a single line.

<br>

### 1.4.3 Living Knowledge: Bringing Internal Assets Back to Life

Dormant internal materials are revived as the document's core visual assets and accurate references.

- During document writing, the AI itself judges where images are needed and performs searches, surfacing assets sleeping in the existing knowledge repository and inserting them.
- Older materials with degraded image quality are automatically enhanced via an AI Upscaling Engine and instantly turned into high-quality visual assets.

<br>

### 1.4.4 Plug-and-Play Automation: Start Immediately Without Developers

You don't have to be a developer. There is no longer any need to manually configure MCP servers or carry out complex pre-setup and API integration as in the past.

- **One-Click Skill Deployment:** Core logic has been separated into independent libraries and wrapped with Claude Skills.
- **Universal Adoption:** Without complex server configuration, simply attaching a single 'Skill' to an agent makes it work immediately — letting non-developer teams instantly complete and roll out their own company-specific agents without IT department assistance.
